IT Solution Digital

An online computer store in Phnom Penh, making it easy to find, compare and buy laptops, monitors and software licences.

The challenge

IT Solution Digital is a computer shop in Phnom Penh with a huge catalogue: laptops and desktops from Apple, ASUS, Dell, HP, Lenovo, MSI and Microsoft Surface, plus monitors, printers, networking gear, servers and licensed software. Customers needed to find the right product quickly and buy online with confidence, and businesses and schools needed a clear route to bulk and education deals.

The approach

I built the store around search and browsing, so customers can get to a product in one or two clicks:

  • Search first: a large “Search for anything” bar sits at the top of every page, next to Wishlist, Cart and Login.
  • Mega-menu by need: Desktops, Laptops (by brand) and License Software (Adobe, Autodesk, Microsoft, Canva, SketchUp and more), with Clearance, Education Sector, Rent & Lease and Today’s deal one click away.
  • Shop by brand: a scrolling strip of brand logos under the hero banner.
  • Fast product cards: image, name, price and both “Add to cart” and “Buy now” buttons, grouped into rows for laptops, monitors, mini PCs, software and printers.

Building trust

Buying expensive tech online needs reassurance. Every page ends with clear promises (100% original products, a 30-day return window, shipping and secure payment), and customers can reach sales directly by phone or Telegram, the way people in Cambodia prefer to talk to shops.

Built to manage

The store runs on WordPress with WooCommerce and an Elementor-based theme, so the team can add products, change prices, run promotions and swap the homepage banners themselves.

The result

The store is live at itsolutiondigital.com, with a full catalogue, cart and checkout, and dedicated paths for education and business customers.
