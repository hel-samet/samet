TrueVisions International School

A bilingual website for an international school in Phnom Penh, keeping parents up to date across 12 campuses.

Visit live site ↗

truevisions.edu.kh
Client
TrueVisions International School
Role
Design & development
Platform
WordPress
Languages
English & Khmer

The challenge

TrueVisions International School of Cambodia runs 12 campuses and teaches from kindergarten through high school, with programmes adapted from Singapore, the US, New Zealand, Australia and England. Parents needed one place to understand the school, its services, and what is happening week to week, in both English and Khmer.

The approach

I organised the site around the questions parents actually ask, with a short, clear navigation:

  • About Us & Curriculum: who the school is and how it teaches.
  • Services: Study Abroad, Kindergarten, Secondary School, High School, School Bus and Day Care Centre, each with its own page.
  • Events & News, Gallery and Contact Us.

The homepage brings the most important things together: a campus hero, “Hot news”, announcements, key numbers (campuses, teachers, students) and the management team. The bold red brand colour carries through the navigation and headings.

Built for staff to update

The school publishes news often: graduations, partnerships with universities in Australia and New Zealand, new campuses and student achievements. Building on WordPress lets staff post bilingual updates and photos themselves, without a developer.

The result

The site is live at truevisions.edu.kh and is updated regularly by the school team, giving parents a single, reliable source for news and services.
